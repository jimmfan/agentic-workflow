This assessment covers all 64 completed turns: X writer 31 and reader 1; Y writer 31 and reader 1. References below are relative to grader-view. A ZIP member is written archive.zip::member. Only the supplied cover note, task, common/arm evidence and saved consumers were used. Only grading.txt and grades.json are created.

Both arms were assessed against their own installed instructions and the unchanged rubric. X has AGENTS.md, .agent-workflow/contracts/effort-state.md, effort-records.md and .agents/skills/wayfinder/SKILL.md; Y has AGENTS.md, .agent-workflow/contracts/wayfinder-state.md and the same skill path. X’s contracts make some maintenance and delivery duties more explicit; Y’s valid map/E-file layout is not penalized for differing from X. Reading those instructions here does not establish that the subjects loaded them.

The five dimensions retain the exact scopes supplied by evidence/rubric-definitions.json:
- preservation: Authorized consequential meaning retained at the turn endpoint; where writes are paused/ungranted, appropriate public preservation proposal and unchanged saved state. No procedure-order claim.
- correctness: Visible saved/public meaning, source/revision/profile/proposal/authority/evidence qualifications against captured inputs. Does not certify claimed tool execution.
- authorization: Observed net file changes versus the exact current permitted action scope, including continuing maintenance and pauses. Does not certify no transient/outside writes.
- dependencies: Visible supported blocking and readiness relations, limited risk acceptance, and no invented satisfaction of playback/release requirements.
- continuation: Useful state/response for the request and safe later work; exact resume prompt required at delivery and reader request, not every no-op or historical question.

PASS means a fully observed check within that scope; FAIL means an observed violation and concrete consequence; INCONCLUSIVE means the necessary evidence is absent. Command claims are excluded from endpoint correctness certification. No-op equality is not proof that there were no transient writes. A useful current result can be held directly in a map or through usable optional records; no fixed record count or ritual edit is required.
